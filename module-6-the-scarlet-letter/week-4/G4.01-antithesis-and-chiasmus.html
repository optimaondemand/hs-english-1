WEEK 4 · GRAMMAR
Antithesis and Chiasmus
Lesson · matched and mirrored structures
🎯 FOCUS
Hawthorne sets the sainted minister against the woman of the scarlet letter in two short sentences. What makes the contrast hit?
Antithesis puts opposed ideas in matching grammatical form. Chiasmus repeats two terms in reverse order. Both turn a judgment into a pattern the reader remembers.
🎯 OBJECTIVES
1
I can define antithesis and chiasmus.
2
I can identify both structures in sentences from the closing chapters.
3
I can write one antithesis and one chiasmus about the novel.
📋 MATERIALS

📚 PART 1 · WARM-UP
💭 WARM-UP
Which version is easier to remember?
A. Hester stood in the market-place, shamed, while Dimmesdale stood in the church, honored.
B. “The sainted minister in the church! The woman of the scarlet letter in the market-place!”
📚 PART 2 · THE LESSON

Antithesis sets opposed ideas side by side in parallel grammatical form. Two things must be true: the ideas are opposed, and the form is matched (a phrase against a phrase, a clause against a clause). A pair of opposites in loose, unmatched wording is a contrast, not an antithesis.

Chiasmus (ky-AZ-mus) repeats two terms and reverses their order: A B, then B A. The name comes from the Greek letter chi, which is shaped like an X, the crossing the terms make.

TWO TESTS
Antithesis: opposed ideas in matching form. Chiasmus: the same two terms, swapped in the second half.
ON THE BOARD · SPOT THE PATTERN
ANTITHESIS · FROM THE REVELATION CHAPTERS
“at once a shadow and a splendor”
The sainted minister in the church! The woman of the scarlet letter in the market-place!
CHIASMUS · PRACTICE MODELS, NOT HAWTHORNE’S
She wore the letter, and the letter wore her.
He kept his secret until his secret kept him.
The town judged Hester; in time, Hester judged the town.
WRONG, THEN RIGHT
✗ Hester was shamed in public, but the minister was honored although he had sinned too.
✓ The town shamed the sinner it could see and honored the sinner it could not.
✗ She wore the letter, and she was changed by the letter.
✓ She wore the letter, and the letter wore her.
The gold underline marks the matched or swapped parts. In the antitheses the match is in the grammar; in the chiasmus the match is in the words themselves, reversed.

➡️ PRACTICE

Write one antithesis about Dimmesdale in the pulpit and in private. Write one chiasmus about Hester and the town. Match the form in the antithesis; swap the same two terms in the chiasmus.
