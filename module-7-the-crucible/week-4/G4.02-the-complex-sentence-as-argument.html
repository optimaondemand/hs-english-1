The Complex Sentence as Argument
Lesson · claim in the main clause
📖 FOCUS
A claim and the point it concedes can share one sentence. Which clause should carry which?
The main clause asserts; the dependent clause grants, explains, or limits. The thesis, topic sentences, and counterclaim of the project essay are all built this way.

📚 PART 1 · WARM-UP
💭 WARM-UP
Which sentence puts more weight on the claim that fear drives the court?
A. Although Danforth believes the court serves justice, the play shows that fear drives its decisions.
B. Danforth believes the court serves justice, although the play shows that fear drives its decisions.
📚 PART 2 · THE LESSON

A complex sentence has one independent clause and at least one dependent clause. In an argument, the independent clause carries the claim; the dependent clause carries what the writer grants, explains, or conditions. The choice of clause is a choice of emphasis.

Job of the dependent clauseOpening wordsEffect
Concessionalthough, even though, whileGrants a point, then asserts the claim.
Reasonbecause, sinceGives the claim its ground.
Conditionif, unlessLimits when the claim holds.
THREE RULES
Put the claim in the main clause. Put a comma after a dependent clause that opens the sentence. Do not let a dependent clause stand alone as a sentence: that is a fragment. Limit a sentence to one or two dependent clauses.
ON THE BOARD · WRONG, THEN RIGHT
Flat: Danforth believes the court serves justice. The play shows that fear drives its decisions.
Complex: Although Danforth believes the court serves justice, the play shows that fear drives its decisions.
Claim in the wrong clause: The Pardoner preaches against greed although he is driven by it.
Claim in the main clause: Although the Pardoner preaches against greed, he is driven by it.
Fragment: Because Proctor tears up his confession.
Complete: Because Proctor tears up his confession, the hanging goes forward.
Reason: Hester’s silence reads as loyalty because it protects another person at her own cost.
The gold underline marks the dependent clause. The main clause carries the claim.

The same shape serves three argument sentences: the thesis (concession plus claim), the topic sentence (reason plus claim), and the counterclaim (Although some readers hold…, then the reply).

✎ YOUR TURN · CHOOSE THE BEST ANSWER

2. Which is a complete sentence?
3. Which version puts the claim in the main clause?
➡️ APPLY

Write your thesis as one complex sentence: a concession in the dependent clause, the claim in the main clause. Then write a topic sentence for one body paragraph in the same shape.

💪 THE ONE THING TO CARRY FORWARD

The main clause is the claim. Grant the opposing point in a dependent clause, then assert.
