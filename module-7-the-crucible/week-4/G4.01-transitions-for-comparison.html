Lesson · sentences that compare
📖 FOCUS
Two texts in one paragraph are not yet a comparison. What makes the relation visible?
Transitions state whether the next idea matches or opposes the last. The cross-text essay depends on them in every body paragraph.

📚 PART 2 · THE LESSON

A transition tells the reader how the next idea relates to the last. Two relations matter in a cross-text essay: similarity (similarly, likewise, in the same way, both) and contrast (however, in contrast, on the other hand, yet, whereas, unlike). Without one, two texts placed in the same paragraph are stacked, not compared.

RelationTransitionsPattern
Similaritysimilarly, likewise, in the same way, alsoClause; likewise, clause.
Contrasthowever, in contrast, on the other hand, neverthelessClause; however, clause. Or a new sentence: However, clause.
Contrast (subordinating)whereas, while, althoughWhereas clause, clause. No semicolon.
ONE RULE TO REMEMBER
A conjunctive adverb (however, likewise, in contrast) cannot join two clauses with a comma alone. Use a semicolon before it and a comma after it, or start a new sentence. A subordinating word (whereas, while) needs no semicolon.
ON THE BOARD · WRONG, THEN RIGHT
Stacked: Gawain hides the girdle. Proctor will not let a false confession stand.
Compared: Gawain hides the girdle to save his life; Proctor, in contrast, will not let a false confession stand to save his.
Comma splice: Hester protects Dimmesdale, however, she bears the shame alone.
Fixed: Hester protects Dimmesdale; however, she bears the shame alone.
Subordinator: Whereas Electra acts to avenge her father, Chrysothemis urges obedience to the household.
Similarity: Elizabeth lies to protect her husband; likewise, Hester stays silent to protect the minister.
The gold underline marks the transition. Red lines show the two common errors: stacked sentences and the comma splice.

While can mean “at the same time” or “in contrast.” In a comparison, whereas carries only the second meaning and avoids confusion.

2. Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
3. Which revision turns the stacked sentences into a comparison? Electra plans revenge. Hester accepts punishment.
➡️ APPLY

Write two sentences comparing how two course texts handle your chosen thread. Use one similarity transition and one contrast transition, each punctuated correctly.

💪 THE ONE THING TO CARRY FORWARD

A transition names the relation. Choose it deliberately, and give it a semicolon and a comma when it joins two clauses.
