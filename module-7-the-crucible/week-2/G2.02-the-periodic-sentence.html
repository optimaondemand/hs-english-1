GRAMMAR
The Periodic Sentence
Lesson
🎯 FOCUS
A periodic sentence holds its main clause until the end.
A loose sentence states its point first and adds detail after. A periodic sentence builds through modifiers and subordinate clauses, and the sentence is incomplete until the final words. The form creates suspense in drama and weight in argument.
🎯 OBJECTIVES
1
I can tell a periodic sentence from a loose one.
2
I can revise a loose sentence into periodic form.
3
I can use the form to state an argument and know when to leave it out.

📚 PART 1 · WARM-UP
💭 READ BOTH ALOUD
A. John turned to Mary Warren and demanded that she testify, after the needle was found and Elizabeth was led away.
B. After the needle was found and Elizabeth was led away, John turned to Mary Warren and demanded that she testify.
Which one makes you wait?
📚 PART 2 · THE LESSON

A loose sentence can end after its main clause and still make sense. A periodic sentence cannot: its meaning is suspended until the last clause or phrase. The test is simple. Cover the end of the sentence. If the part you can see is already a complete statement, the sentence is loose. If the part you can see leaves the reader waiting, it is periodic.

ONE RULE TO REMEMBER
Put the most important clause last and everything it depends on first. Use the form once in a paragraph, not in every sentence.
ON THE BOARD · LOOSE, THEN PERIODIC
NARRATIVE
LOOSE  John Proctor turned to Mary Warren and demanded that she testify, after the needle was found, after the warrant was read, and after Elizabeth was led out of the house.
PERIODIC  After the needle was found, after the warrant was read, and after Elizabeth was led out of the house, John Proctor turned to Mary Warren and demanded that she testify.
ARGUMENT
LOOSE  A reader can take the play as a warning about fear, since the court believes accusation over evidence, since neighbors turn on neighbors, and since reputation counts for more than proof.
PERIODIC  Since the court believes accusation over evidence, since neighbors turn on neighbors, and since reputation counts for more than proof, a reader can take the play as a warning about fear.
THESIS FRAME
Although [a view the argument must acknowledge], and although [a second view], [the claim].
The gold underline marks the suspended part. The main clause comes after it, and the sentence is not complete before then.
