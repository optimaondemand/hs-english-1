Polish and title
Three sweeps and a title that works twice
●
Polish in three sweeps, one error family each. Three targeted passes catch more than five general read-throughs.
1
Dialogue joints
Commas and periods inside the closing quotation mark. A new paragraph for each new speaker.
2
Tense drift
Read only your verbs. Stay in one home tense unless a flashback is a choice.
3
Pacing
Read aloud. Hurry where tension is high. Cut where you get bored.
QUICK CHECK · NOT GRADED
Which sentence has drifted out of the past tense?
●
Your title is your first planted detail. It should work twice: it promises something concrete before the story, and it means more after it. The masters' titles point at an object, an event, or a question. None of them name the moral.

Write the craft reflection
Three techniques, where, and who taught you
●
After your story, write one paragraph of 150 to 250 words under the heading Craft Reflection. Name at least three techniques you used, say where each one appears in your story, and say which studied story taught you each. Use at least three Word Study terms accurately.
THE FRAME
One technique I used is ______. It appears in ______. I learned it from ______, where ______. Another technique is ______.

Choose three Word Study terms to use in your reflection.

4
Write the Source Note
Three lines at the end of your reflection
●
End your craft reflection with a Source Note. It is three lines. Writers look things up, and this says what you did.
1
One thing you looked up
A place, object, job, period detail, or a word's real meaning.
2
Where you found it
Cited in MLA. If it came from JSTOR, use the Cite option on the article page.
3
How it changed a detail
If checking it changed nothing, look up something that matters to a sentence you wrote.
WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE
“I needed my character to send an urgent message in 1912, so I checked how telegrams actually worked and found that they were charged by the word. That is why her message in paragraph four is so short and strange, and why her brother misreads it. The clipped telegram style became the whole misunderstanding the story turns on.”

Three or four sentences is plenty.
