Answer the objection, then close
A counterclaim and a conclusion
●
A counterclaim is the strongest objection to your thesis, stated so fairly that its supporters would agree with your wording. Then you reply. A weak version of the objection lets you win too easily, and your teacher will notice.
A MODEL COUNTERCLAIM (THEME: HONESTY)
A reader might say that Hester's silence is not hiding at all, since she wears her sin in public, so she does not belong with Gawain and Elizabeth. That is partly true: she hides one fact, not her whole self. Even so, the one fact she hides is the center of her story, and it costs her what the lies cost the others.

3
Show the comparison, and cite it
Transitions and MLA, the 20-point row
●
Two texts in one paragraph are stacked, not compared, until a transition names the relation. For similarity, use likewise, similarly, or in the same way. For contrast, use however, in contrast, or whereas. A word like however needs a semicolon before it and a comma after it when it joins two clauses.
STACKED, THEN COMPARED
Stacked: Gawain hides the girdle. Proctor will not let a false confession stand.
Compared: Gawain hides the girdle to save his life; Proctor, in contrast, will not let a false confession stand to save his.
QUICK CHECK · NOT GRADED
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
