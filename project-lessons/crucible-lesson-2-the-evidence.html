Project Feeder 2: Cross-Text Evidence, 15 points, due in Week 3
PURPOSE

Build an evidence chart that ties one moment from The Crucible, one from each of two earlier course texts, and one idea from the researched source to the theme chosen in Feeder 1.

STEPS
  1. Copy your theme (justice, loyalty, family, or duty) and your three texts from Feeder 1.
  2. Choose one moment from each text that shows the theme at work.
  3. Record each moment as a short quotation or a close paraphrase, with its location.
  4. Choose one idea from your researched source and record it with its MLA in-text citation.
  5. Write one or two sentences on what each entry shows about the theme.
  6. Add one sentence that connects the four entries.
THE CHART

Use four rows. Quote the play only from your own copy, one sentence at most, and give the act and page. Quote other texts exactly as they appear in your edition. The sample row shows the shape on a different theme and text than most projects will use.
