2
Write your thesis
One complex sentence that answers your research question
●
A thesis climbs three rungs. A topic is a label: loyalty in three texts. An observation is true and cannot be disputed: characters protect people they are loyal to. A thesis takes a position: each text shows a protector who pays for the protection, and each judges the payment differently. Your thesis names all three texts.
A MODEL, ON A DIFFERENT THEME
Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, The Scarlet Letter, and The Crucible each show a person who hides a truth to protect someone, and each text suggests that the hiding costs the hider more than the person it was meant to protect.
Your thesis
One sentence. Name all three texts.

A MODEL OPENING (THEME: HONESTY)
What do you owe the truth when telling it would hurt someone you love? Gawain hides a belt from his host, Hester hides a name from her town, and Elizabeth Proctor hides a fact from a court. Each hides to protect. This essay asks what the three texts, read together, say about the person who hides. In all three, the hiding costs the hider more than the person it was meant to protect.

7
Draft Body 1
200 to 260 words: one idea, two texts, one point where they differ
●
A body paragraph opens with your topic sentence, then moves through a moment from each text. After each moment, explain it. End on the point where the texts differ. Use a comparison word to show the relation: likewise, in contrast, whereas.
A MODEL BODY PARAGRAPH (THEME: HONESTY, SHORTER THAN YOURS)
Hiding a truth to protect a person has a price, and each text shows who pays it. Gawain keeps the green girdle from his host to save his own life, and he leaves wearing a belt that he later calls a mark of his own failure. Elizabeth Proctor, likewise, tells one lie in court to shield her husband, and that lie helps seal his fate. Hester, in contrast, hides nothing about her own sin; she refuses only to name the man who shares it, so she carries the town's contempt alone. In all three, the protection holds for a moment, and then the cost comes back to the person who kept the secret.
