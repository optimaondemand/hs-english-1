Weave in the source and check MLA
The source belongs inside your argument, not beside it
●
A source that is stacked beside your argument sits in its own paragraph and never meets your thesis. A source that is woven in shows up in the middle of your reasoning, with an in-text citation, and helps prove a point you make.
