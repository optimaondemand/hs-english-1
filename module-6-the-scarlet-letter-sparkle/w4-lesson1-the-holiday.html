The Holiday

Chapters XX–XXII
Header: Mary Hallock Foote, engraving for the 1878 edition.
Read aloud
Focus

The minister comes home from the forest with a plan, and the town turns out for a holiday. What has changed, and who can see it?

Objectives
  • Trace what the minister wants to do and what he does on the way home.
  • Explain what the shipmaster’s news changes for Hester.
  • Compare how Hester, Pearl and Mistress Hibbins see the minister on Election Day.
  • Quote and cite exactly, like this: (Ch. XXII).
