Three Lenses

Claim
  • Is it clear which speaker at the hearing you write about?
  • Does the claim say how that person argues, not only what they said?
Quotation
  • Is the quotation exact, with its chapter cited like this: (Ch. VIII)?
  • Does the sentence after it explain what it shows?
Sentences
  • Does one sentence have an absolute phrase, a noun plus a participle, set off by a comma?
  • Do the items in your series share one form?
