Chapters XII and XXIII
Header: Mary Hallock Foote, engraving for the 1878 edition.
Read aloud
Focus

The novel climbs the same wooden steps three times. Who stands there, who watches, and what can be seen each time?

Objectives
  • Compare the three scaffold scenes by who stands, who watches, the hour and the light.
  • Follow Pearl’s question from Chapter XII to Chapter XXIII.
  • Quote exact lines and cite them by chapter.
  • Explain what changes when the watchers change.

Before you reread
Your reading task
  1. Read Chapters XII and XXIII.
  2. Mark every line that tells who is on the platform or who is watching. Select the line and choose Evidence.
  3. Note in the margin who it is and what they can see from where they are.
Example

“they would have discerned no face above the platform, nor hardly the outline of a human shape, in the dark gray of the midnight.” Ch. XII
