Chapters VII–VIII
Header: Mary Hallock Foote, engraving for the 1878 edition.
Read aloud
Focus

The town wants to take Pearl from Hester. Who speaks for the child, who speaks against her, and whose words win?

Objectives
  • Explain how Pearl’s dress and the Governor’s breastplate show the scarlet letter in new forms.
  • Tell apart the four voices at the hearing and what each one argues.
  • Explain what Pearl’s answer about the rose-bush does to the hearing.
  • Say what the end of the hearing lets a reader infer, and what it leaves unsaid.
  • Connect Mistress Hibbins’s invitation to the chapter’s last sentence.
