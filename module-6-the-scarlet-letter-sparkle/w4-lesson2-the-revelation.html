Focus

The procession comes out of the church at noon, and afterward the town cannot agree on what it saw. What happens, and what is left for the reader to decide?

Objectives
  • Put the noon scene in order.
  • Say what each person in that scene does.
  • Describe what becomes of Chillingworth, Pearl and Hester.
  • Read the last image of the novel.

Before You Read

The sermon is over and the ship sails tomorrow. What do you expect the minister to do when the procession comes out of the church? Why? 2–3 sentences.
Your reading task
  1. Read Chapters XXIII–XXIV.
  2. Mark every line where someone explains what happened on the scaffold, or what it meant. Select the line and choose Evidence.
  3. Note in the margin whose explanation it is.
Example

“This earthly faintness was, in their view, only another phase of the minister’s celestial strength” Ch. XXIII

MarginThe crowd. They see him weaken and call it holiness. They explain what they see to fit what they already believe.
