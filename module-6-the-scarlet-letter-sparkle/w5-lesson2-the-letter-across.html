Focus

For seven years and more the scarlet letter goes wherever Hester goes. Where does it go, and what does she do with it?

Objectives
  • Follow the letter to four places in the novel and read each passage closely.
  • Compare what Hester does with the letter in different chapters.
  • Tell who comes to whose door in Chapter XIII and in Chapter XXIV.
  • Quote and cite exact lines.
