Focus

Seven years have passed. Who has changed: the town, Hester, or the man who watches her?

Objectives
  • Compare how the people, the rulers and Hester read the letter after seven years.
  • Describe how Chillingworth has changed, in the text’s own words.
  • Explain whom Hester blames and whom Chillingworth blames.
  • Track Pearl’s questions and Hester’s answers about the letter.
  • Quote and cite exactly, like this: (Ch. XIII).

Before You Read

Seven years can change how a town sees a person. What would make a town soften toward someone it once shamed, if that person never asked it to? 3–4 sentences.
Your reading task
  1. Read Chapters XIII–XV.
  2. Mark every line that shows what someone now thinks the scarlet letter means: the people, the rulers, Chillingworth, Pearl, or Hester herself. Select the line and choose Evidence.
  3. Note in the margin who is reading the letter and what they think it means.
